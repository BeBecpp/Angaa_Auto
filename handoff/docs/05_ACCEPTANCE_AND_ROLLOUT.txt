M4 — FINANCE, SERVICE PLANS AND REMINDERS
Deliver invoice issuance, partial/full manual payments and reversals, maintenance plans, consent/preferences, outbox/local simulation, operational reports.
Pass: integer arithmetic with fractional quantities and documented rounding; posted total ignored; issued invoice preserved; partial payment gives correct balance; duplicate reference/idempotency does not double count; reversal correct and audited; total reversed <= settled payment. Cash received and billed revenue remain distinct.
Clock-controlled reminder cases:
• Date-only due, km-only due, both OR; unknown mileage does not invent trigger.
• Stale older mileage cannot mark a new plan due; decreasing km needs correction workflow.
• Re-running evaluation creates one stage message, not duplicates.
• Two workers cannot claim one outbox entry simultaneously.
• Consent revoked before dispatch skips the queued message.
• Changed phone/ownership/rebooked appointment invalidates old recipient/version.
• Quiet-hours shift preserves business timezone and skips no-longer-useful reminder.
• Confirming a booking inside 24h skips past reminder stage; never sends both stale stages at once.
• Simulated driver stays simulated; provider acceptance stays accepted, not delivered.
• Timeout after send produces unknown/reconciliation, no blind duplicate retry.
• Failed known-not-sent delivery backs off; stale/satisfied plan cancels future reminders.
• No real SMS transport or production credentials are used by tests.

ONE COMPLETE DEMO SCRIPT
1 Guest opens /, selects service/unknown problem, describes symptom, chooses gallery/camera photo and submits preferred time.
2 Owner logs into /admin, triages request and identifies customer/vehicle safely, confirms available bay/mechanic time.
3 Customer sees confirmed appointment through verified account. Another account cannot see it.
4 Mechanic checks in car, records km and condition photos, diagnoses and publishes permitted findings through advisor workflow.
5 Advisor sends estimate revision 1. Customer approves it in portal. Mechanic begins allowed work.
6 Additional work creates revision 2; revision 1 approval does not cover added scope. Customer approves or declines new work.
7 Mechanic records work and after photo; permitted staff perform QC; order ready.
8 Issue invoice; record partial then final manual payment, verify balance; deliver and print summary.
9 Customer opens own photo history. Staff creates appropriate due date/km plan and records reminder consent.
10 Advance test clock, run reminder dry-run/log driver, inspect simulated outbox result, rebook. Nothing is actually sent.
11 Verify cancelled appointment/reminder, opt-out, ownership change, concurrent slot conflict and restore of sample DB+photo backup.
