CORRECTNESS AND TRUST
1. A public request is pending, not a confirmed appointment. Staff confirmation creates/reserves resources transactionally. Include idempotency on submit and conversion.
2. Booking conflicts are prevented on the server under concurrency, including mechanics and bays. Lock stable resource rows before checking overlaps. Validate opening hours, holidays, duration and buffers. Editing/dragging uses the same action and reverts on conflict.
3. Vehicle plate and phone are identifiers for staff search, not authorization. No public history lookup by plate+phone. Verify phone ownership and authorize every customer record and media request. Never auto-attach an unverified public request to an existing customer vehicle.
4. Estimate revisions are immutable once sent. Customer approval applies to exactly the displayed revision and amount, with audit evidence. New scope/cost needs new approval. Phone approvals must be recorded as staff-recorded phone approvals, not electronic signatures.
5. Separate repair status, appointment status, invoice/payment state, and message delivery state. Completed/delivered is not synonymous with paid. Block unapproved repair work except controlled, reasoned owner override.
6. Use private media, actual server image validation/decoding, random paths, bounded pixel count, EXIF orientation and stripping; secure expiring downloads. Keep pre/post photos and visibility flags. Do not publish customer repair photos as marketing without separate approval. HEIC conversion must be real and capability-checked; otherwise provide honest fallback guidance.
7. Maintenance reminders are date OR measured-km based, per item and owner policy, never a universal invented schedule. Do not pretend to read mileage remotely. Use an outbox/idempotency design, consent checks, quiet hours, retries, timeout reconciliation and cancellation of stale scheduled messages. Log simulation is never delivery. Real provider adapters require verified contracts and credentials.
8. Money uses integer MNT and server-calculated totals. Issued financial records and approved estimates retain snapshots. No fake payment gateway or ebarimt claim. Cash/bank/manual payment records work locally; QPay and ebarimt are later adapters only if requested.
9. Audit sensitive actions; apply roles/policies server-side, CSRF, rate limits, safe exports and non-PII logs. Customer links must not leak access to another customer. Show the app's local demo banner only in local/testing environments.
